We have never had a customer's server actioned as a result of an order. Members and boosts are delivered through your public invite — we never need admin rights, a bot in your server, or your account credentials. If you want to be extra careful, use the custom join rate on member orders so arrivals are gradual rather than a sudden spike.

We never ask for your password, your token, admin permissions, or server ownership — and you should refuse anyone selling Discord growth who does. Set your invite to never expire with unlimited uses so delivery is not interrupted.

Real members are genuine Discord accounts that join and can participate. Online and offline members are presence accounts that raise your visible member and online counts — they make a server look established but they will not chat. Pick real members if you want engagement; pick online or offline if you only need the counts to look healthy.

Orders start within minutes of payment confirmation. Members arrive as fast as possible by default, but you can set a custom join rate at checkout — for example 50 members every 15 minutes — to spread it over hours or days. Boosts and Nitro activate as soon as the order is processed.
